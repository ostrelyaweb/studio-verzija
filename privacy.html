02 Principles & Architecture of Restraint

In accordance with Article 5 of the GDPR, Ostrelya adheres strictly to the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, and data minimization (privacy by design and by default):

  • Self-Hosted Assets: All web fonts, JavaScript libraries, and stylesheets are hosted locally on our own European servers. No connections to external CDNs or font servers (such as Google Fonts or cdnjs) are initiated when you load this website, eliminating unauthorized visitor IP transmission.
  • No Mandatory Tracking: We do not deploy behavioral ad networks, cross-site trackers, session replayers, or surveillance fingerprinting scripts.
  • Data Minimization: We only collect personal details that you voluntarily submit through direct inquiries, and process only technical connection telemetry strictly required for server integrity.

We process personal data solely within the limits established by Article 6(1) of the GDPR. The specific processing categories include:

When visiting our website, your browser automatically transmits technical metadata required for the transmission of web pages. This data includes: IP address (anonymized or truncated where supported), date and time of request, requested URL path, HTTP status code, transferred byte volume, browser type and operating system version.

  • Purpose: Ensuring server stability, routing HTTP requests, detecting network anomalies, and defending against DDoS and malicious attacks.
  • Legal Basis: Legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (securing web infrastructure integrity).
  • Storage Duration: Server log files are automatically rotated and overwritten within [7 to 14 DAYS — HOSTING DEFAULT] unless retained temporarily for documented security incidents.

B. Project Inquiries & Direct Communication

When you submit a message through our Project Inquiry form or send an email to hello@ostrelya.com, we collect your full name, work email address, company or studio name (if provided), service interest, and message content.

  • Purpose: Evaluating your project requirements, preparing commercial proposals, responding to your queries, and negotiating service agreements.
  • Legal Basis: Pre-contractual measures taken at the data subject's request pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and our legitimate business interest in responding to incoming B2B communications pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Storage Duration: Inquiry records are retained for the duration of the pre-contractual discussions. 05 Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, you hold fundamental rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is processed and to request a copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17 GDPR): You may request deletion of your personal data where statutory grounds exist.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): You can request restriction of processing under specified conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): You have the right to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@ostrelya.com or hello@ostrelya.com. We will respond without undue delay and at the latest within one month of receipt.
