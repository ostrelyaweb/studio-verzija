Cookie Policy

Transparent disclosure of storage technologies, telemetry, and cookie categories used on this website in compliance with Directive 2002/58/EC and the Croatian Electronic Communications Act.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version: 1.0 • Framework: EU ePrivacy & GDPR
Instant Consent Control

You can review, modify, or revoke your cookie choices at any time with immediate effect.

01 What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies include HTML5 Local Storage (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`), which allow web applications to store information locally within your browser.

At Ostrelya, we believe privacy is an integral element of digital craft. We deliberately design our digital flagships to function with minimal reliance on persistent identifiers and zero mandatory third-party tracking.

03 Categories & Legal Framework

A. Strictly Necessary (Exempt from Consent)

Under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC and Article 138 of the Croatian Electronic Communications Act (Zakon o elektroničkim komunikacijama), storage that is strictly necessary to deliver a service explicitly requested by the subscriber or user is exempt from the requirement of prior consent.

We use `ostrelya_consent` exclusively to document and respect your consent choices across sessions. This token contains no personal identifiers, tracking hashes, or cross-site references.

B. Analytics & Telemetry (Consent Required)

Analytics tools provide statistical insights into how our pages perform, viewport dimensions, and page rendering latency. These scripts are strictly dormant by default and are only initialized if you click "Accept all" or toggle "Analytics & Performance" to active within the CMP modal.

C. Marketing & Advertising

Ostrelya Studio does not display third-party advertisements or use remarketing pixels (such as Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel) on this website. Should marketing campaigns be activated in the future, they will remain disabled until explicit opt-in consent is provided.

04 Self-Hosted Architecture (Zero Third-Party Leaks)

Many websites inadvertently transmit visitor IP addresses to third parties via external CDNs (such as Google Fonts or public script repositories). Ostrelya strictly self-hosts all fonts, scripts, stylesheets, and animations. Loading our website initiates connections exclusively with our own secure European server infrastructure.

05 Managing Storage in Your Browser

In addition to our on-site Consent Manager, all major web browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies and local storage tokens. Please refer to your browser's documentation:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences / Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

06 Contact for Privacy & Cookie Inquiries

If you have any questions about our use of storage technologies, please reach out to privacy@ostrelya.com or inspect our comprehensive Privacy Notice.
