PROJECT 02 / 04 Product Website • HealthTech & AI
VANGUARD MEDICAL AI — MUNICH / LONDON

Human Progress Amplified

Demystifying autonomous clinical telemetry for top European hospital networks while preserving clinical gravity, scientific elegance, and uncompromising software velocity.

  • WCAG 2.2 AA Accessible Clinical Data Charts & Screen Reader Mapping
  • Modular Design Token System with High-Density Layout Modes
  • Headless CMS Integration for Clinical Whitepapers & Trials
  • Sub-second Edge Deployment Across European Health Zones
Engineering Metric

Steady 60fps WebGL execution on standard clinical tablets with GPU-saving battery throttling.

PROJECT 03 / 04 Campaign Website • Luxury Horology
KRONOS HOROLOGY — GENEVA, SWITZERLAND

Beyond Boundaries

An asymmetric, kinetic digital showroom celebrating the launch of a limited-edition mechanical chronometer, capturing the tactile microscopic tolerances of luxury Swiss horology.

The Challenge & Execution

To unveil a hand-finished tourbillon timepiece limited to 50 numbered editions worldwide, Kronos needed a sensory experience that reflected the horological precision of Geneva workshops. We engineered a scroll-choreographed interactive narrative using mathematical Bézier vector tracers and specular letter glints that guide collectors through caliber mechanics to private reservation.

Scope & Deliverables
  • Scroll-Driven Choreography via GSAP Timelines
  • Microscopic Caliber Vector Animations & SVG Tracers
  • Letter-Strict Specular Lighting & Shader Accents
  • Private VIP Collector Reservation Concierge Interface
  • Full prefers-reduced-motion Accessible Fallback Architecture
Commercial Result

100% allocation of 50 timepieces reserved within 48 hours • International design recognition.
