# Ostrelya About and Journal Pages

## Approved direction

The two supplied concept images are the visual specification. The pages reproduce their editorial hierarchy, spacing and first-viewport composition instead of expanding them into conventional multi-section agency pages.

## About

The About page is a single architectural hero: studio eyebrow and oversized statement on the left, an Ostrelya construction mark with grid, orbit, signal line and focal point in the center, and the studio positioning copy on the right. A four-part information rail closes the viewport. The center mark is a genuine WebGL model constructed from the exact vector coordinates supplied in the Blender generator: both split ring arcs and the arrow are separately extruded, shaded and depth-tested. Pointer, touch and keyboard input rotate the model freely; inertia and restrained idle motion keep the interaction tactile. A static image remains available if WebGL cannot initialize.

## Journal

The Journal page uses the approved publication layout: masthead, one dominant article on the left, and two compact entries on the right. Article numbering, metadata, serif headlines, monochrome architectural imagery and the electric-blue accent reproduce the reference. The previously proposed filter bar and additional article grid are intentionally removed.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop preserves the exact three-column About and two-column Journal compositions. On smaller screens, content stacks in the same reading order without horizontal overflow. Navigation, semantic headings, alternative image text and reduced-motion behavior remain accessible.
